Why Choose Us

An import house,
not a marketplace.

Three commitments shape every order we accept. They are why our oldest clients have been with us for over a decade, and why every house we represent renews its contract on a handshake.

01 — Sourcing

Authenticated at origin

We visit every house we represent. Stock is verified, lot-numbered, and sealed in the country it was made.

02 — Logistics

Sealed in transit

Climate-controlled containers from Dubai, Manchester, and Surabaya. Every shipment carries a chain-of-custody dossier.

03 — Heritage

Houses of significance

From a 130-year English toffee maker to a fifth-generation Arabian perfumer. The brands we carry have earned their place — we are merely the bridge.

A Note from the Founder

We don't make. We choose. Every house we carry was visited at origin, every shipment opened and inspected. Our work is to find the makers worth knowing — and to make sure what arrives at your door is exactly what left their hands.
